Hydro Jetting for Fairmount Properties

The town identifies Fairmount as part of Camillus and describes its business corridor along West Genesee Street. Homes and businesses sit close together here, and the drain load on a restaurant, a shop and a house on the same street can look very different.

A business corridor brings one extra question to a drain call. Is this a household problem, a commercial one, or a shared line? The answer decides who needs to be involved and what the cleaning should aim to remove.

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour grease, scale and roots from a sound pipe wall. For a line that carries commercial kitchen waste or heavy daily use, grease is often the first thing to check, and an inspection shows how far it has built up.

  • Whether neighboring properties see the same symptoms

Hydro Jetting Situations Along a Business Corridor

Kitchens that serve customers

A small food business can load a line with grease faster than a household can. Jetting strips the layer off the wall rather than opening a narrow path.

Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Fairmount

Is Fairmount its own town?

The town identifies Fairmount as part of Camillus, which is how this page treats it.

Does a business corridor change how a drain is cleaned?

It can. Commercial use, shared lines and grease loads all affect the plan, and the inspection should account for them.

I only have one slow sink. Is that serious?

It may be a local clog rather than a main-line issue. Several fixtures affected at once point toward the main line.

Can hydro jetting prevent grease backups?

Where an inspection shows grease buildup, planned cleaning can remove it before it blocks the line. Whether that makes sense depends on what the camera finds.

What if neighbors have the same problem?

That can point to a shared or public line. Tell the crew and report it to the municipality as well.

What details should I give when I request service?

List the affected fixtures, when the problem started, and anything that changed around that time. Mention any past cleanings or repairs, and where the cleanout is if you know.

How is jetting different from snaking?

A snake opens a path through a blockage, while jetting scours the pipe wall with high-pressure water. For residue that keeps causing repeat clogs, jetting addresses what snaking leaves behind, when the pipe's condition allows.

Do I need an inspection before jetting?

Yes. The cause of the blockage decides the method, and a cracked or weak pipe can be made worse by high pressure. Inspection first is the rule for any property.

Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Amboy-Belle Isle

Does the area's aviation history say anything about my drains?

No. It is local history and not evidence about a private pipe. Only records and an inspection can speak to your line.

What should I do before calling?

Note which fixtures are slow, when it started and where your cleanout is. That information speeds up the inspection.

If a cleaning only helps for a while, why?

Something is probably still building up or growing in the line. Ask whether roots, grease or scale is the cause.

Does hydro jetting fix cracks?

No. It removes an obstruction. A cracked or shifted pipe needs a repair assessment.

Is hydro jetting right for every home?

No. The crew should check the line first and tell you whether the method fits.

What details should I give when I request service?

List the affected fixtures, when the problem started, and anything that changed around that time. Mention any past cleanings or repairs, and where the cleanout is if you know.

How is jetting different from snaking?

A snake opens a path through a blockage, while jetting scours the pipe wall with high-pressure water. For residue that keeps causing repeat clogs, jetting addresses what snaking leaves behind, when the pipe's condition allows.

Do I need an inspection before jetting?

Yes. The cause of the blockage decides the method, and a cracked or weak pipe can be made worse by high pressure. Inspection first is the rule for any property.
